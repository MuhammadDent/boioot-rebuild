I want you to improve the **pricing configuration UX and business logic** in the admin plan editor for Boioot, with minimal safe changes and without breaking existing plan behavior.

## Context

In the admin dashboard, when editing plans such as:

* **Advanced plan for property seekers**
* other plans that may support different purchase models

the pricing section currently has poor UX and shows price inputs too early.

---

## Main business rule to implement

### 1) One-time purchase plans must NOT show monthly/yearly pricing

For plans that are purchased **one time only**:

* do **not** show:

  * monthly price
  * yearly price
  * recurring subscription pricing UI
* instead show only the relevant one-time purchase price field

This applies specifically to the **advanced plan for property seekers** if its purchase model is one-time.

---

## UX improvement required

### 2) Price options should appear only AFTER selecting purchase model

Right now the pricing fields are shown before the admin clearly chooses the purchase type / subscription type.

I want the flow changed to:

1. Admin first selects the plan purchase model, such as:

   * one-time purchase
   * monthly subscription
   * yearly subscription
   * both monthly and yearly
   * or whatever valid models the current system supports

2. Only after that selection:

   * show the relevant pricing inputs
   * hide irrelevant pricing inputs

### Expected behavior examples

#### Case A: One-time purchase

Show:

* one-time price only

Hide:

* monthly price
* yearly price
* recurring subscription prices list

#### Case B: Monthly subscription only

Show:

* monthly price only

Hide:

* yearly price
* one-time purchase price

#### Case C: Yearly subscription only

Show:

* yearly price only

Hide:

* monthly price
* one-time price

#### Case D: Monthly + Yearly

Show:

* monthly price
* yearly price

Hide:

* one-time purchase price

---

## Design / UX expectations

### 3) Improve the pricing section layout

The pricing area should feel cleaner and more logical.

I want:

* purchase model selection first
* pricing fields second
* conditional rendering based on selection
* no confusing display of irrelevant fields
* a more professional visual hierarchy

Please improve:

* spacing
* labels
* grouping
* visibility logic
* section order

without redesigning the whole modal unnecessarily.
